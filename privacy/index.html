Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

On this page

    This Privacy Policy explains how Ghosty ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information required to provide its features on Discord.

    #1. Information We Collect

    Ghosty only collects information necessary for its proper operation.

    This information may include:

    • Discord User IDs.
    • Discord Server IDs.
    • Discord Channel IDs.
    • Discord Role IDs.
    • Server configurations and settings.
    • Bot preferences configured by administrators.
    • Information required for notification systems.
    • Technical logs used for debugging and improving the service.

    #2. Information We Do Not Collect

    Ghosty does not intentionally collect:

    • Passwords.
    • Email addresses.
    • Payment information.
    • Sensitive personal information.
    • Private messages unless explicitly required by a feature.

    #3. How We Use Information

    Collected information is used exclusively to:

    • Provide Ghosty's features.
    • Save server configurations.
    • Manage notification systems.
    • Fix errors and improve performance.
    • Prevent abuse of the service.

    #4. Data Sharing

    Ghosty does not sell, rent, or share collected information with third parties.

    Information may only be disclosed when required by law or requested by a competent authority.

    #5. Data Retention

    Information is stored only for as long as necessary to provide the service or until deletion is requested.

    #6. Data Deletion

    Server administrators may request deletion of their server's data by contacting the Ghosty support server.

    Data will be removed within a reasonable period unless retention is required by law.

    #7. Security

    Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, loss, or modification.

    However, no system can guarantee complete security.

    #8. Third-Party Services

    Ghosty may integrate with external services to provide certain features, such as notification systems or other integrations.

    The use of these services may also be subject to their own privacy policies.

    #9. Changes to This Policy

    This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time due to new features, legal requirements, or improvements to the service.

    The "Last Updated" date will indicate the current version.

    #10. Contact

    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, you can contact the developer through the official Ghosty support server.
